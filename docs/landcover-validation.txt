Why this check exists: NASA FIRMS detects heat, not forest loss. In the Amazon
burning season most fires are on land that was already cleared, so a fire-based
deforestation tool has to demonstrate its targets are actually on forest. This
is that demonstration. WorldCover predates the fires by five years, which is the
baseline we want: land that was already cropland or grassland in 2021 could not
be undergoing deforestation in 2026.
